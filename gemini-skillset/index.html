02

Grounded in the standards.

Not generic templates — anchored to the frameworks the industry measures, contracts and reports against. That is what makes a number defensible in a review.

RICS NRM

Order‑of‑cost estimating and elemental cost planning (NRM1), detailed measurement and bills of quantities (NRM2), whole‑life cost (NRM3).

RIBA Plan of Work

Estimate precision and stage gates align to the eight stages, 0 Strategic Definition through 7 In Use — the number matures with the design.

NEC4 + CVR

Earned value aligned to the Accepted Programme and Defined Cost, integrated with cost‑value reconciliation in one monthly report.

Market benchmarking

Cost per m² by market and construction‑inflation indices (ICMS‑style) — adjusted for time and place before anything is compared.

03

How it runs in Gemini Spark.

  • 01
    The sandbox has no network.Code only computes over files — pandas, openpyxl, scipy, reportlab. All input and output to Workspace is a Gemini tool call, never the code.
  • 02
    No memory, no scheduler.Persistence lives in a Sheets config tab. Anything scheduled — a monthly report, an on‑submit trigger — is generated as Apps Script.
  • 03
    Skills compose.Each skill declares its task and its tools; Spark applies the right one automatically and chains skills together into a full pipeline.

Credibility is the asset.

Every skill carries the same discipline: distinguish actual from forecast, never report a metric with a missing input, never trim contingency to make a number close, flag assumptions. The pack is built to protect the adviser's credibility — to be defended in the room, not to dress a number up.
