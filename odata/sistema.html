ODATA QR03 · Los Cues, El Marqués · Turner & Townsend

One source of truth for the whole project

Today every team runs like its own small company: their own process, their own formats — and the only way to know whether someone else's work affects yours is to knock on their door. This system connects every department on Microsoft — capture once, and everyone sees real progress in real time.

Built on: Dataverse · Power Platform Frameworks: AWP · Earned Value · Lineage Build: ~2 months Full rollout: 4–6 months

01 The problem, in three symptoms

All three are the same disease: there is no single source of truth with traceability. Each team produces its own version of reality, and nothing reconciles them.

Isolation

They don't connect

Each team reports its own way and shares nothing. Finding out about a change in another area means going to ask — and if that person isn't there, it waits until the next day.

Capture

Everyone captures differently

There is no standard method. The same reality is recorded seven ways, and none of them feeds a common dashboard.

Loss

Information dies

What gets resolved gets resolved; the rest is lost in an email or a WhatsApp. By the end of the project there is no record to measure or learn from.

03 The departments

Each department owns a data domain (its System of Record) and consumes the others. The system gives it, in real time, what it has to go chase today.

PMO

Project Management

Integrates, prioritizes and reports to the client. Stops chasing versions: it runs the reconciliation and builds the report as a query over the data.

Owns: project structure, decisions, integrated report
Cost Management

Cost Management

Records financial progress tagged as financial, without pretending it is physical. The gate reconciles it against site progress.

Owns: cost, earned value, valuations
Planning

Planning

Publishes the schedule and work packages (AWP). Releases work only when physical confirms the constraints are cleared.

Owns: schedule, AWP packages, dates and sequence
Quality

Quality

Raises non-conformances and inspections with a code and a status. Every finding is traceable to its closure — not lost in a stray form.

Owns: NCRs, inspections, quality checklists
MEP

Mechanical · Electrical · Plumbing

Captures the physical progress of its systems at the point of installation. It is the source of truth for its work-in-place, tied to the BIM model.

Owns: physical progress and issues for MEP systems
CSA

Civil · Structural · Architectural

Same as MEP for civil, structure and architecture: verified installed quantities, not estimated, tied to their package and element.

Owns: physical progress and issues for CSA works
Document Control

Document Control

Governs documents, versions, transmittals and RFIs. Ties each site point to its document and model — the lineage between the paper and the steel.

Owns: documents, versions, RFIs, transmittals
External

General Contractors

They are not inside the tenant, but they submit their progress and invoices through a controlled form. Their number enters tagged and gets reconciled — detail below.

Delivers: progress estimates + invoices

04 External contractors (General Contractors)

The challenge: GCs are external and today report in their own format (the 40% that doesn't match anyone). The solution doesn't put them inside the tenant — it gives them a controlled door.

Form

Estimates portal

A Power Apps Portal (or a Power Pages form) where the GC captures progress by front/package. It enters Dataverse tagged by dimension and owner, ready to reconcile against site physical.

Billing

Invoice upload

The GC uploads its invoice (PDF/XML CFDI) through the same portal. A Power Automate flow reads it, ties it to its estimate and package, and sends it to Cost's approval gate. Nothing arrives by stray email.

The result: the GC keeps working outside, but its progress and its money enter the same model, with a code and traceability, and are validated against site reality instead of accepted blindly.

05 The end of "40% vs 25% vs 80%"

The General Contractor reports 40%, Supervision 25%, Cost 80%. They are not lying — they measure different dimensions, and no one tags it. The system separates the two truths:

Physical
Verified work-in-place. Credited by site (MEP/CSA), and only when the package meets its AWP rule: installed, not estimated.
Financial
Earned value / cost. Credited by Cost Management on its own base, without pretending it is physical progress.

Every number carries its tag and its owner. When financial runs ahead of physical, the system flags the discrepancy and sends it to review — the follow-up check from whoever is closest to reality, which doesn't exist today.

Data

Capture

Power Apps

Automation

Power Automate

Ingestion bot, approvals, and Process Mining (Pro).

Report

Power BI

Live dashboards. The client's format comes out as a query.

The differentiator

If we capture at the right granularity — each point with its code, dimension, owner and link to the package and model — the specific format the client asks for stops mattering: it is generated as an equation over the data, not a report someone rebuilds by hand. We own the granular truth; the reports are derivations.

07 The roadmap · 4 to 6 months

Paced to your build. About two months to design and build, ready to go live the moment your next construction stage begins — and fully embedded by month six. The clock starts on approval and data access.

  1. Weeks 1–4 · BuildFoundation & pilotData model in Dataverse + the mobile capture app, with two pilot departments to prove physical-vs-financial reconciliation on real data.
  2. Weeks 5–8 · BuildFull platformThe rest onboard the same model — PMO, Planning, Quality, MEP, CSA and Document Control — plus the GC portal for estimates and invoices, and Power BI dashboards for management and client. Deliverable: a working platform on real project data.
  3. Month 3 · the anchorGo live with the new stageThe next construction stage begins — and the platform is its backbone from day one. A clean start, no old mess carried in, with each department trained as they pick it up, and operating manuals delivered.
  4. Months 4–6 · EmbedDay-to-day, team by teamRolled out into daily operations across all eight teams — reconciliation, earned value and Process Mining keeping themselves up to date until it is the daily single source of truth. Full adoption.
